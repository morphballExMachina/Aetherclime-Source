# Nexus category, tags and disclosure — RC1 draft

**Proposed main category:** Utilities (because the framework is a shared runtime service), with **Modders Resource** and **Utilities for Modders** tags. Final category choice depends on the options available for this Skyrim SE submission.

**Starting tags shown in the author UI:**

- `Plugins: Script Extender`
- `Skyrim Script Extender (SKSE)`
- `Modders Resource`
- `Utilities for Modders`
- `AI-Generated Content`
- `AI Media` where AI-generated page descriptions or images are included (Nexus 2026 policy includes mod-page descriptions)

Do **not** tag Aetherclime as a weather replacement, performance optimizer, compatibility patch or visual season mod. It observes and models environment data rather than changing the visible weather, textures or LODs.

**AI disclosure:** The main project source implementation, tests and public documents were generated with substantial AI assistance under human direction, iterative build/runtime testing and design review. The appropriate primary label is `AI-Generated Content` rather than `AI Assisted` (which Nexus reserves for comparatively limited assistance). Include a short candid disclosure in the listing, without apology or debate. Follow the Nexus permission selector for AI training, forks, and derivatives; the repository's MIT source license does not implicitly grant rights to third-party assets.

**Reference (checked November 24, 2026):** https://help.nexusmods.com/article/180-ai-policy-changes-2026

**Proposed metadata:**

- Title: **Aetherclime - Environmental Framework (SKSE)**
- Tagline: **Aetherclime understands the environment. Other mods bring it to life.**
- One-line description: **A read-only SKSE environmental framework for Skyrim: calendar seasons, regional climate, indoor temperatures, geography and a native API. No ESP/ESL/ESM required.**

**Presentation:** Weathered-gold/dark-slate dividers are optional. Keep section titles accessible and high-contrast; do not imply rendered snowfall or seasonal meshes are provided by the framework. Attribution, screenshots and mod-manager installer UI checks remain author sign-off tasks.
